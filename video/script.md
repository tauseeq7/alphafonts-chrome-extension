# Voiceover script (optional)

The video has on-screen captions but no sound. Recording your own voice makes it much more engaging. Read each part while the video plays. The word count is chosen to fit the time in each part at a relaxed pace (about 2.3 words per second).

Tip: record in a quiet room with your phone or a headset mic, then add the audio track in YouTube Studio's editor, CapCut or any video editor.

| Time | What is on screen | Say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 to 0:05 | Logo and title | "Ever wondered what font a website is using? This is AlphaFonts Font Inspector." |
| 0:05 to 0:16 | Popup, Start Inspecting, then hovering | "Click the button in your toolbar and press Start Inspecting, or use Alt Shift F. Then just hover over any text to see its font, size, weight and color." |
| 0:16 to 0:25 | Locking and copying CSS | "Click a text to lock the panel. Press Copy CSS, and paste the styles straight into your code." |
| 0:25 to 0:35 | Edit values, slider | "Want to try a different size? Press Edit values and drag the slider. The page changes live, and one click puts it back." |
| 0:35 to 0:49 | Contrast checker | "Is the text readable? The contrast checker tests the color against the real background. If it fails, one click applies a color that passes. You can copy only the changes you made." |
| 0:49 to 0:59 | Labels and scrolling | "Or label every text element at once, and see every font and size on the page. The labels follow you as you scroll." |
| 0:59 to 1:04 | Remove layers | "Remove layers clears everything and undoes your edits." |
| 1:04 to 1:10 | Closing card | "It's free, private, and runs on your computer. Find it for Firefox, Opera and Edge, and more font tools at alphafonts dot com." |

Note: if you say "Chrome" anywhere, remember the Chrome Web Store version is not published yet.
