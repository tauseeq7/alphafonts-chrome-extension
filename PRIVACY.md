# Privacy Policy: AlphaFonts Font Inspector

_Last updated: 14 October 2026_

AlphaFonts Font Inspector does not collect, store or transmit any personal data or browsing data.

- **What it reads:** only after you start it (button, shortcut or right-click menu), it reads the font and style information of the text you point at on the page you are viewing. This happens entirely inside your browser.
- **What it saves:** only your own settings (theme, which properties to show, and a few switches), using your browser's extension storage on your own computer. Nothing else is saved.
- **What it sends:** nothing. The extension makes no network requests of its own. It has no analytics, no tracking, no advertising and no accounts, and it loads no code from the internet.
- **Clipboard:** when you press a copy button, the copied text is placed on your clipboard and goes nowhere else.
- **Links:** the "AlphaFonts" links open https://alphafonts.com in a new tab only when you click them.
- **Permissions:** `activeTab` and `scripting` let it run on the current tab when you ask; `contextMenus` adds the right-click item; `storage` saves your settings.

Questions: https://alphafonts.com
